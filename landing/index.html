How it works

Four rounds, no black box.

  1. 01

    Submit your decision

    Describe the call you need to make. No forms, no wizard — write it like you'd brief a real board.

  2. 02

    The board deliberates

    CEO, CFO, CTO, and CMO agents reason through it in rounds, live, each pushing back on the others' blind spots.

  3. 03

    Reports land with alignment scores

    Every agent produces a written report and a number: how aligned they are with where the deliberation is heading.

  4. 04

    You get a board decision

    A final, written call, plus the full transcript of how the board got there — nothing hidden.

The board

Four executives. Four blind spots covered.

CEO

Chief Executive

Strategy & the final call. Weighs the decision against where the company is actually trying to go.

CFO

Chief Financial

Runway & unit economics. Asks what this costs, and whether you can afford it twice.

CTO

Chief Technology

Feasibility & risk. Asks what breaks, and what it actually takes to build it for real.

CMO

Chief Marketing

Positioning & demand. Asks who wants this, and whether they'll pay for it.
